RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement is made as of January 15, 2027, between Landlord
and Tenant for the property located at 123 Main Street, Santa Ana, California.

1. TERM. The initial term shall commence on March 14, 2027 and end on March 13, 2028.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay rent of $2,000 per month, payable on the first of each
month. Tenant agrees rent shall increase annually on the first anniversary of the
commencement date by 10 percent.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,000. Landlord may
increase the security deposit on the first anniversary of the commencement date by
an amount not to exceed 10 percent.

4. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and maintenance under
$500, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical, and appliance repairs.
Landlord shall be responsible only for structural repairs exceeding $2,400.
Effective on the first anniversary of the commencement date, the tenant's
maintenance responsibility threshold shall increase from $500 to $800.

5. LATE FEE. A late fee of $100 shall be assessed if rent is not received within
5 days of the due date. Late fee shall increase to $150 on the first anniversary
of the commencement date.

6. RENEWAL. This lease shall automatically renew for an additional 12-month term
upon expiration of the initial term, unless Tenant provides written notice of
non-renewal at least 60 days prior to expiration. Rent upon renewal shall
increase by 10 percent over the then-current rent.

7. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
