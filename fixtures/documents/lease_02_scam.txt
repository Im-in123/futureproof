RESIDENTIAL SUBLEASE AGREEMENT

This Sublease is made as of January 1, 2027, for the property located at
456 Market Street, San Francisco, California.

1. TERM. The initial term shall commence on March 14, 2027 and end on March 13, 2028.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $3,500. Rent shall increase annually on the first
anniversary of the commencement date by 18 percent, and on each subsequent
anniversary by 15 percent, 20 percent, and 22 percent respectively for years
three, four, and five.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,500. The Landlord
may increase the security deposit on each anniversary of the commencement date
by 15 percent.

4. LATE FEE. A late fee of $500 shall be assessed if rent is not received within
1 day of the due date. Late fee shall increase by 25 percent on each anniversary
of the commencement date.

5. RENEWAL. This Sublease shall automatically renew for successive 12-month terms
at a 14 percent increase over the then-current rent, unless Tenant provides
written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to expiration.

6. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and maintenance under
$2,000, including structural repairs.

7. FRANCHISE FEE. Tenant agrees to pay a one-time franchise licensing fee of
$12,000 upon execution of this Sublease, non-refundable.
